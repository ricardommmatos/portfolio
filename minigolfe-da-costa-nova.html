The goal for Minigolfe da Costa Nova was to grow its audience on Facebook and Instagram and get more people interacting with the venue online. I defined the digital strategy, built the website and managed social media, paid campaigns and email marketing.

Project

Minigolfe da Costa Nova

Scope
Digital strategy, website, social media, advertising and email marketing

Social media and advertising

I worked with photographers and video producers to improve the quality of the content. I created photo carousels, videos and Instagram Stories about minigolf and events at the venue, and ran giveaways on Facebook and Instagram to encourage participation.

I tracked follower growth, engagement and likes on both platforms. The project also included Facebook Ads and Google Ads campaigns.

Email marketing

I sent regular newsletters with updates and offers from the venue. Free admission on a visitor’s birthday was used as an incentive to attract new contacts.

Results

  • Over 14,000Facebook followers
  • +70%Increase in visitor numbers
